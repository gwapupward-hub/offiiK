# Source Policy

## Purpose
This file defines additional sourcing rules for the Muʿāmalāt Expert plug-in.

## Primary evidence
All Qur’an, hadith, Companion reports, and classical legal evidence must be verified through the Islamic Teacher Core.

## Contemporary sources
For modern products, prioritize:

1. Original product terms, contracts, disclosures, white papers, and regulatory documents.
2. International Islamic Fiqh Academy resolutions.
3. AAOIFI Sharīʿah standards.
4. Islamic Fiqh Council resolutions.
5. Established fatwa councils.
6. Qualified scholars specializing in Islamic finance.
7. Reliable academic and industry research explaining product mechanics.

## Required distinction
Always distinguish:

- revealed evidence,
- classical juristic application,
- contemporary council resolution,
- individual scholarly opinion,
- and the skill’s own inference.

## Prohibited sourcing
Do not rely primarily on:

- anonymous blogs,
- social-media clips,
- marketing claims,
- AI summaries without verification,
- unsourced halal labels,
- or affiliate content promoting a financial product.

## Product documents
A ruling based on a modern product must be based on its actual current terms. Product names alone are insufficient.
