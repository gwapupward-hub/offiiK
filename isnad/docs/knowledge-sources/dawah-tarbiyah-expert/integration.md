# Daʿwah & Tarbiyah Expert integration

## Load order

1. Load the Islamic Teacher Core.
2. Load Daʿwah & Tarbiyah Expert for outreach strategy, audience-sensitive
   teaching, convert care, mentorship, spiritual formation, correction, or
   community-program design.
3. Load Tafsīr, Hadith Sciences, ʿAqīdah, Fiqh, Seerah, or Arabic Language as
   peer specialists when the request also requires interpretation,
   authentication, doctrine, legal derivation, historical reconstruction, or
   linguistic analysis.
4. Apply the Core and shared evidence policy whenever instructions conflict.

## Routing

Route requests that explicitly ask about daʿwah, tarbiyah, inviting someone to
Islam, prospective or new-Muslim support, shahādah follow-up, teaching Islam,
halaqahs, mentoring, spiritual-growth plans, youth or family formation, masjid,
campus, prison, workplace, or online outreach, correction etiquette, enjoining
good, debates, public religious communication, pastoral boundaries, volunteer
training, safeguarding, or Islamic community-program design.

Do not route generic business outreach, secular mentoring, software onboarding,
or ordinary questions that merely mention Islam. The primary request must
concern Islamic invitation, teaching, formation, correction, pastoral care, or
community development.

## Division of specialist authority

```text
Islamic Teacher Core > shared source and hadith policy
```

- Daʿwah & Tarbiyah Expert owns outreach strategy, teaching sequence,
  mentorship, follow-up, formation, and community-development design.
- Tafsīr Expert owns intended Qur'anic interpretation and revelation context.
- Hadith Sciences Expert owns report authentication and wording.
- ʿAqīdah Expert owns doctrine, doubts, and takfīr boundaries.
- Fiqh Expert owns rulings, obligations, prohibitions, and legal consequences.
- Seerah Expert owns historical reconstruction and Prophetic chronology.
- Arabic Language Expert owns linguistic analysis and translation.

Cross-domain answers may use several modules, but daʿwah strategy must never
authorize coercion, vigilantism, humiliation, spiritual abuse, amateur
diagnosis, or overreach into another specialist's authority.
