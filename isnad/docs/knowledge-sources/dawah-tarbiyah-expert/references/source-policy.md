# Source Policy

## Purpose

Use this policy for evidence-heavy daʿwah, tarbiyah, curriculum, and correction work. Inherit the Islamic Teacher Core's hierarchy and verification standards.

## Source Order

1. **Qur'an** — Verify Arabic wording, verse number, context, and a recognized translation. Use Tafsīr Expert for interpretive disputes.
2. **Authentic Sunnah** — Verify collection, wording, narrator, grade, and context. Use Hadith Sciences Expert for disputed or complex reports.
3. **Companions and early generations** — Distinguish Prophetic reports from Companion and Successor statements. Verify attribution.
4. **Recognized Sunni scholarship** — Use works of tafsīr, hadith, fiqh, creed, spirituality, education, and daʿwah within their fields.
5. **Reliable contemporary expertise** — Use qualified pastoral, educational, safeguarding, medical, psychological, sociological, or legal expertise for its proper subject.

Do not turn a management, psychology, or marketing technique into Sunnah merely because it appears useful.

## Foundational Qur'anic Anchors

Research and apply relevant passages, including:

- calling with wisdom, good instruction, and the best manner of discussion;
- calling upon insight;
- gentleness and consultation;
- gentle speech even toward a tyrant;
- no compulsion in religion;
- the caller's duty to convey and Allah's control of guidance;
- teaching oneself and one's family;
- mutual counsel in truth and patience;
- cooperation in righteousness;
- brotherhood, reconciliation, and guarding honor.

Verify the exact verse and interpretation before quoting. Do not detach a command from its audience, context, or juristic limits.

## Foundational Sunnah Themes

Research verified reports concerning:

- making matters accessible without repelling people;
- gentleness beautifying conduct;
- religion as sincere counsel;
- conveying verified knowledge;
- reward for guiding to good;
- gradual teaching and priorities;
- consistency in deeds;
- correcting errors without unnecessary humiliation;
- mercy to young people and respect for elders;
- personal example, patience, consultation, and service.

Do not quote a blended paraphrase as a hadith. When two reports support one paragraph, cite each report separately.

## Prophetic Example

Use Seerah Expert to verify historical cases before presenting them as universal methods. Distinguish:

- a reliably established event;
- a context-specific political or judicial act;
- a personal pastoral response;
- a general teaching principle;
- and a later author's reconstruction.

Do not use a weak popular story because it is emotionally effective.

## Educational and Pastoral Evidence

When adopting contemporary practices:

- identify whether the claim is empirical, clinical, educational, legal, or religious;
- use competent sources for that domain;
- avoid pseudoscience and viral statistics;
- distinguish measured outcomes from anecdotes;
- preserve local law and professional ethics;
- state uncertainty and population limits.

Modern expertise may improve delivery and safeguarding but cannot override revelation.

## Citation Rules

- Cite verses by sūrah and verse.
- Cite hadith by collection and verified locator; attribute grading when material.
- Name the author and work for scholarly positions.
- Identify a policy, study, or dataset precisely for current factual claims.
- Label paraphrases and summaries.
- Never fabricate page numbers, Arabic quotations, narrations, conversions, or success rates.

If verification fails, say that the claim could not be verified and omit it from the lesson or strategy.
