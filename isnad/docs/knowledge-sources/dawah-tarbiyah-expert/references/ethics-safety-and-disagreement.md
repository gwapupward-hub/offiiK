## Sincerity and Truthfulness

Do not claim to know a person's sincerity. Audit observable incentives:

- Is content shaped to please Allah and benefit people, or primarily to win status?
- Are mistakes corrected?
- Are sources shown?
- Are numbers and stories verified?
- Are limitations admitted?
- Are private people turned into content?

Avoid fake miracles, embellished conversions, fabricated science claims, staged debates, and selective edits.

## Consent and Coercion

Religious invitation must be voluntary. Coercion can arise through:

- physical threats;
- family or marital pressure;
- employment, grades, housing, immigration, food, or aid;
- prison or institutional privilege;
- public shame;
- romantic attention;
- financial dependency;
- fear of losing the community.

Do not exploit unequal power even when the person verbally agrees.

## Power and Boundaries

Teachers and mentors must not:

- pursue romantic or sexual access through spiritual authority;
- request private images or unnecessary intimate details;
- create exclusive late-night dependency;
- borrow from or pressure learners for money;
- claim special revelation or guaranteed spiritual insight;
- demand obedience beyond a legitimate role;
- retaliate against criticism or departure.

Use supervision, transparent communication channels, role rotation, and complaint mechanisms.

## Confidentiality and Safeguarding

Keep ordinary disclosures private, but never promise absolute secrecy.

Escalate through appropriate channels when there is:

- imminent danger;
- child or vulnerable-adult abuse;
- credible self-harm or violence risk;
- trafficking or exploitation;
- serious criminal conduct requiring lawful reporting.

Share only what is necessary with those responsible for safety. Preserve evidence lawfully. Do not launch public accusations or amateur investigations.

## Money and Fundraising

- State the purpose, recipient, fees, restrictions, and financial controls.
- Separate spiritual pressure from donation requests.
- Do not imply that giving to a particular leader guarantees blessing, healing, or salvation.
- Protect donor privacy.
- Use independent accounting and conflict-of-interest controls.
- Do not divert zakāh or restricted funds.
- Route zakāh and contract rulings to Fiqh Expert.

## Disagreement and Takfīr

Distinguish:

- essentials and secondary questions;
- established error and recognized disagreement;
- a statement in the abstract and judgment of a person;
- advice, scholarly refutation, organizational discipline, and legal judgment.

Never declare a named Muslim outside Islam. Use ʿAqīdah Expert for doctrinal principles and Fiqh Expert for legal consequences. Refer individual cases to qualified scholars with the facts.

Do not use labels as substitutes for evidence. Avoid loyalty tests around personalities, organizations, parties, or disputed secondary issues.

## Public Controversy

Before posting:

1. verify the facts and exact words;
2. seek context and, where safe, clarification;
3. distinguish public harm from gossip;
4. identify the proper authority or safeguarding route;
5. disclose uncertainty;
6. protect victims and private data;
7. correct proportionately;
8. avoid pile-ons and monetized outrage.

Public allegations of abuse, crime, or apostasy require especially careful legal, ethical, and scholarly handling.

## Safety Escalation

When danger is immediate:

- prioritize physical safety;
- contact appropriate emergency or protective services;
- involve a trusted local person when safe;
- avoid confronting a dangerous person alone;
- preserve relevant evidence;
- refer to qualified legal, clinical, and scholarly help.

Do not give tactical instructions for violence, vigilantism, evading lawful investigation, or retaliation.
