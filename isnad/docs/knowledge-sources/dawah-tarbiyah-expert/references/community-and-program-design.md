## Curriculum Architecture

Sequence:

1. prerequisites and foundations;
2. essential knowledge and practice;
3. guided application;
4. revision and feedback;
5. companionship and service;
6. next-stage pathways.

For each unit define a learner outcome, verified evidence, activity, practical application, and assessment. Keep advanced disputes out of beginner curricula unless needed to prevent a known harm.

Offer modular entry points for learners who cannot attend every session.

## Team and Governance

Assign explicit roles:

- scholarly oversight;
- program lead;
- teachers and mentors;
- safeguarding lead;
- operations and data;
- communications;
- referrals;
- finance;
- evaluation.

Use consultation, documented decisions, conflict-of-interest disclosure, role limits, and removal procedures. No single charismatic leader should control doctrine, money, complaints, and discipline.

Train volunteers before placing them with vulnerable people. Supervise and debrief them.

## Safeguarding and Operations

Maintain:

- screening appropriate to the role;
- conduct standards;
- minor and vulnerable-adult protections;
- gender-appropriate and power-aware boundaries;
- incident and disclosure procedures;
- emergency contacts;
- privacy and data retention rules;
- transportation and venue safety;
- online moderation;
- complaint and appeal routes;
- lawful mandatory reporting.

Do not investigate serious abuse internally beyond immediate safety, preservation, and proper referral.

## Outreach Funnel and Follow-Up

Use an ethical pathway:

```text
Awareness → permission-based contact → useful encounter → question pathway
→ relationship or class → tailored support → community belonging → service
```

At each transition:

- obtain consent;
- explain what happens next;
- collect minimal data;
- allow an easy exit;
- protect privacy;
- follow through.

Do not repeatedly contact someone who has declined.

## Community Health

Build more than lectures:

- welcoming teams;
- small groups;
- family support;
- convert care;
- youth pathways;
- service projects;
- conflict resolution;
- financial transparency;
- scholar access;
- professional referrals;
- leadership succession.

Watch for cliques, ethnic exclusion, class barriers, gender neglect, gossip, burnout, and dependence on one speaker.

## Measurement and Improvement

Combine quantitative and qualitative evidence:

- reach and opt-in contact;
- attendance and retention;
- completion and comprehension;
- practice support;
- belonging and safe relationships;
- referrals completed;
- mentor reliability;
- incidents and response quality;
- learner feedback;
- service and leadership development.

Protect privacy and explain data use. Do not publish vulnerable stories or conversion counts without informed consent.

Review failures openly:

1. What outcome was missed?
2. What evidence explains it?
3. Was the program unsafe, inaccessible, poorly taught, or badly timed?
4. What should stop, change, or continue?
5. Who must be consulted?
6. When will the change be reviewed?
