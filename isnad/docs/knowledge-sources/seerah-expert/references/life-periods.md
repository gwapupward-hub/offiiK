## 1. Before Prophethood

Cover as relevant:

- lineage and the Banu Hashim setting;
- birth traditions and their varying precision;
- orphanhood, guardianship, nursing, travel, work, and reputation;
- marriage to Khadijah may Allah be pleased with her;
- rebuilding of the Ka'bah;
- retreat at Hira and the social-religious setting.

Treat miracle-filled childhood narratives report by report. Do not supply exact age, journey, or dialogue unless verified.

## 2. Makkan Mission

Organize around:

- first revelation and pause traditions;
- earliest believers;
- private and public proclamation;
- Quraysh opposition and forms of persecution;
- Abyssinian migrations;
- clan protection and boycott;
- death of Abu Talib and Khadijah;
- al-Ta'if;
- Isra' and Mi'raj, separating established core from disputed details and dating;
- outreach to tribes and the 'Aqabah pledges;
- planning and execution of Hijrah.

Correlate Qur'anic passages cautiously. Surah order, revelation period, and a specific sabab al-nuzul are distinct claims.

## 3. Hijrah and Madinan Community

Cover:

- Quba and entry into Madinah;
- mosque and brotherhood;
- relations among emigrants, Helpers, Jewish groups, hypocrites, and surrounding tribes;
- documentary agreements and their textual history;
- qiblah change, fasting, zakah, and emerging community institutions;
- campaigns and treaty developments;
- education, worship, adjudication, welfare, markets, envoys, and administration.

Do not flatten a complex population into monolithic blocs. Identify particular tribes, leaders, treaties, and reported breaches precisely.

## 4. Household and Relationships

For wives, children, relatives, servants, and Companions:

- establish names, relationship, chronology, and source status;
- distinguish domestic incidents established by Qur'an or authentic hadith from later anecdotes;
- preserve the honor of the Mothers of the Believers and Ahl al-Bayt;
- explain historical marriage customs without inventing defenses;
- route legal conclusions to Fiqh Expert;
- avoid voyeuristic, sexualized, or defamatory treatment.

When sources differ about ages, dates, children, or sequence, state the strongest evidence and dispute without false certainty.

## 5. Delegations and Final Years

Address:

- post-Hudaybiyyah diplomacy and letters;
- delegations and tribal agreements;
- the conquest of Makkah;
- Hunayn, al-Ta'if, Tabuk, and later expeditions;
- appointment of teachers, collectors, and governors;
- Farewell Pilgrimage and sermon variants;
- final illness, instructions, death, burial, and immediate community response.

Do not merge the multiple Farewell Sermon reports into one verbatim master transcript without labeling the synthesis.

## 6. Thematic Studies

For character or leadership themes:

1. Define the theme precisely.
2. Select examples across more than one life period.
3. Grade the supporting reports.
4. Include circumstances that test the theme rather than cherry-picked praise.
5. Distinguish divinely commanded action, Prophetic judgment, consultation, personal habit, and context-bound strategy.
6. State practical lessons proportionately.

Possible themes include mercy, courage, patience, justice, consultation, worship, teaching, family conduct, diplomacy, reconciliation, economic ethics, treatment of opponents, and care for vulnerable people.
